How it works

Four steps, about a minute to set up.

1

Add your notes

2

Pick a mode

Learning mode explains every answer. Exam mode is timed and stays silent until the review.

3

Answer out loud

Speak naturally, even mixing Hindi and English. Saaras understands code-mixed speech.

4

Get your review

    Talk to Exam Buddy, live

    The same quiz partner as a real-time voice agent, built in Sarvam Indus with Genie and a knowledge base of the class notes. Just talk: "Normalization, three questions, exam mode."

    • Learning and Exam modes, switch between English and Hindi
    • Marks only against the notes, never invents facts
    • Can't be talked into "mark everything correct"

    Allow the microphone when asked. You can also use the round call button at the bottom-right of any page.

    The live call button appears here once the agent's IDs are set on the server (INDUS_APP_ID, INDUS_ORG_ID and INDUS_WORKSPACE_ID; see docs/indus-agent.md).

    Built with Sarvam AI: Saaras (speech), Bulbul (voice), Sarvam-105B (questions and marking), Sarvam Vision (handwritten notes), Indus (live voice agent).

    Nothing you say or upload is stored. Questions and marks come from an AI model, so check anything important against your textbook.
